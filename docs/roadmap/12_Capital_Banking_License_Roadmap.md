## 1. Executive Vision & The Strategic Rationale

### 1.1 The Ultimate Objective
12 Capital was conceived not as an ephemeral retail brokerage, but as the foundational financial infrastructure of **12 Group**. While an International Business Company (IBC) brokerage operating under a legal opinion and STP connectivity (via Luramic and Kenmore Design) provides the immediate agility to onboard clients, generate cash flows, and establish track record, the long-term endgame is clear:

> **Transition 12 Capital from an offshore market broker into an authorized, regulated International Digital Bank capable of issuing multi-currency IBANs, holding client custody, executing global FX settlements, and serving as the financial engine for 12 Group’s international ecosystem.**

### 1.2 Why Become an International Bank?
1. **Direct Clearing & Independence from Intermediaries:** Corretoras (brokers) are perpetually reliant on external third-party banks and Payment Service Providers (PSPs), who charge high merchant fees (2.5% to 5.0%) and pose de-risking threats. Operating a licensed bank grants direct SWIFT / SEPA / FedWire connectivity.
2. **Deposit Custody & Balance Sheet Expansion:** A bank can accept institutional deposits, issue deposit certificates, and manage treasury yields, expanding revenue from transaction fees to Net Interest Margin (NIM).
3. **Institutional Prestige & Wealth Structuring:** Ultra-High-Net-Worth Individuals (UHNWIs), family offices, and institutional funds require the regulatory protection of a licensed banking institution before depositing multimillion-dollar portfolios.
4. **Synergy with 12 Group:** Directly financing and facilitating cross-border settlements for group entities (12 Energia, 12 Systems, 12 Farm) while maintaining rigorous regulatory ring-fencing.

│  • Minimum Capital: USD 1,000,000 paid-up│  • Minimum Capital: USD 2,500,000 paid-up   │

### 4.1 Correspondent Banking Strategy (The Critical Bottleneck)
The greatest operational challenge for any newly licensed international bank is securing USD and EUR correspondent clearing. To ensure rapid onboarding:
- **Strategy A (Nested Virtual Accounts):** Prior to direct central bank access, partner with Tier-1 European Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) to issue nested virtual IBANs in the bank’s name.
- **Strategy B (Asset-Backed Clearing):** Maintain significant treasury reserves in US Treasuries and AAA sovereign paper pledged as clearing collateral with correspondent banks.
- **Strategy C (Specialized Clears):** Partner with boutique clearing banks in London, Frankfurt, and San Juan specifically chartered to clear for international financial institutions.

### 4.2 Capital Adequacy Strategy
To fund the required USD 1,000,000 to USD 2,500,000 statutory capital:
1. **Internal Brokerage Retained Cash Flows:** Projected to generate $450,000+ across Years 1–3 in pure retained buffer.
2. **12 Group Holding Capital Allocation:** Slide 8 of the 12 Grupo Strategic Blueprint establishes clear capital allocation: 40% of holding dividend receipts allocated to growth and strategic equity.
3. **Strategic Private Placement:** Opening a non-voting preferred equity tranche to allied family offices and institutional BAM (Business as Mission) partners seeking long-term yield and capital preservation.
