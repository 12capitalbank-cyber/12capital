1. Executive Summary & Corporate Purpose

12 Capital Inc. ("12 Capital" or the "Company") is an internationally structured multi-asset brokerage and financial technology firm incorporated as an International Business Company (IBC) in Saint Lucia. Operating as the flagship financial arm of 12 Group Holding (which includes operational renewable solar assets under Detronic Energia and financial technology systems under FactorHub / FactorOne), the Company provides eligible international retail, professional, and corporate clients with institutional access to Foreign Exchange (FX), CFDs on Precious Metals, Commodities, Global Indices, and Cross-Border Wealth Solutions.

Crucially, 12 Capital is already an active, operational brokerage with live trading accounts, real transaction volume, and operational infrastructure. Operating exclusively on a Straight-Through Processing (STP) / Direct Market Access (DMA) model, 12 Capital does not hold speculative market risk or trade against its clients. Orders are routed directly to institutional liquidity providers, spearheaded by Luramic, while client relationship management and compliance verification are powered by Kenmore Design.

The Company's live web platform is deployed and publicly accessible for regulatory inspection at https://12capital.vercel.app.

5. Compliance, AML/CFT & KYC Framework

The Company enforces a rigorous AML/CFT framework structured around FATF 40 Recommendations:

  • Tiered Onboarding: Tier 1 (Passport/ID + 3D facial liveness biometrics); Tier 2 (Proof of address < 90 days); Tier 3 (Source of Wealth/Funds for accounts > $50,000).
  • Sanctions & PEP Screening: Automated screening against OFAC, UN, EU, UK sanctions lists, and global Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) registers.
  • Strict First-Party Rule: Deposits and withdrawals must match the client's verified legal name exactly. Third-party transfers and anonymous instruments are blocked.
  • Geographic Exclusions: Zero onboarding of US Persons (CFTC/SEC compliance), FATF Blacklist/Greylist nations, or domestic Saint Lucia residents.
