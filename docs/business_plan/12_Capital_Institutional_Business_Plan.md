## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose of Account

### 1.1 Executive Summary
**12 Capital Inc.** ("12 Capital" or the "Company") is an internationally structured multi-asset brokerage, market connectivity, and financial technology firm incorporated as an International Business Company (IBC) in Saint Lucia. The Company operates as the premier international financial services arm of **12 Group Holding**, a diversified corporate conglomerate with proven operating assets in renewable energy (**Detronic Energia**, generating real commercial revenues in Brazil's photovoltaic sector) and financial software (**FactorHub / FactorOne**).

Unlike speculative start-ups, **12 Capital is already an active, operational brokerage** facilitating live foreign exchange and CFD market transactions for an established base of international traders. The Company provides eligible international retail, professional, and corporate clients with seamless, low-latency access to global financial markets—including Foreign Exchange / Global Currencies (FX), Contracts for Difference (CFDs) on Global Indices, Commodities (Gold, Silver, Energy), Digital Assets, and Cross-Border Wealth Advisory. 

Operating strictly on a **Straight-Through Processing (STP) / Direct Market Access (DMA)** non-dealing desk model, 12 Capital does not engage in proprietary directional market making. All client order flow is routed directly to Tier-1 institutional liquidity providers, spearheaded by **Luramic**, thereby eliminating broker-client market conflict and market risk from the Company's balance sheet. Client management, regulatory onboarding, and payment processing orchestration are managed through enterprise-grade financial software provided by **Kenmore Design**.

The Company's live web platform is deployed and publicly accessible for regulatory inspection at **https://12capital.vercel.app** (backed by institutional GitHub repository `12capitalbank-cyber/12capital`).

### 1.2 Specific Purpose of the Corporate Bank Account
12 Capital is formalizing this corporate operational bank account to support its ongoing commercial expansion. In alignment with international banking standards, the Company maintains strict fund segregation principles. The prospective account will be utilized exclusively for:
1. **Initial Equity & Working Capital Injections**: Receiving legitimate shareholder capitalization and equity contributions from the parent holding entity (12 Group Holding) and verified Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBOs).
2. **Corporate Operating Expenditures (OPEX)**: Settlement of recurring vendor invoices, including technology licensing fees (Kenmore Design CRM, trading infrastructure), institutional liquidity clearing fees (Luramic), server cloud infrastructure (Vercel, Supabase, AWS), external legal counsel, compliance retainers, and executive salaries.
3. **Institutional Liquidity Collateral Funding**: Transferring corporate operational buffer capital to institutional liquidity provider margin accounts to support execution credit lines.
4. **Corporate Retained Earnings & Tax Neutral Distributions**: Accumulation of net operational broker profits (spread markups and brokerage commissions) and legitimate dividend distributions to holding shareholders in accordance with corporate governance bylaws.

*Note for Bank Compliance:* This operational account will **never** be commingled with speculative trading activities, nor will it be used as an unregulated pool. Client trading deposits are processed through dedicated segregated payment pathways and client margin clearing hubs.

## 4. Order Execution Architecture (STP / Non-Dealing Desk)

### 4.1 Straight-Through Processing (STP) Integrity
A paramount consideration for banking compliance is the elimination of solvency risks associated with "market-making" or "B-Book" internal dealing desks. Market makers take the counterparty side of client trades; in high-volatility events, such brokers face extreme balance-sheet wipeout risk (as evidenced historically during systemic peg breaks).

**12 Capital explicitly operates on a 100% Non-Dealing Desk (NDD) / Straight-Through Processing (STP) execution framework.**
- Every single buy or sell order placed on the 12 Capital trading portal is instantly matched and routed back-to-back to our primary liquidity provider, **Luramic**.
- 12 Capital does not hold proprietary trading inventory, does not trade against its clients, and does not retain market directional risk.
- The Company's commercial revenue is generated solely through transparent transaction fees (spread markups and fixed ticket commissions), creating full alignment of interest between broker, client, and bank.

```

│                 │ <---- │ Execution Ack   │ <---- │ Fill & Clear    │ <---- │  Liquidity Pool)│
└─────────────────┘       └─────────────────┘       └─────────────────┘       └─────────────────┘
```

### 4.2 Liquidity Aggregation & Latency Optimization
- **Primary Liquidity Provider:** Luramic Institutional.
- **Price Feeds:** Aggregated multi-bank and non-bank liquidity streams yielding ultra-tight raw spreads (starting at 0.0 pips on EUR/USD and USD/JPY).
- **Execution Latency:** Sub-millisecond order transmission via low-latency cross-connects hosted in top-tier financial data centers (Equinix LD4 London and NY4 New York).
- **Slippage & Fill Policies:** Execution under Market Order conditions ensuring positive and negative slippage are treated symmetrically without asymmetric dealer intervention.

- Where reasonable suspicion arises, 12 Capital files formal **Suspicious Activity Reports (SAR / STR)** with the appropriate Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) in accordance with Saint Lucia statutory provisions.

## 8. Risk Management, Capital Protection & Exposure Controls

### 8.1 Market Risk Mitigation (Zero Inventory Risk)
Because 12 Capital routes 100% of order volume via STP to Luramic, the Company maintains no unhedged open market positions. Market price fluctuations in underlying currencies or commodities impact the liquidity provider's aggregated books, not 12 Capital’s balance sheet.

### 8.2 Counterparty Credit Risk Mitigation
- Luramic undergoes ongoing financial health reviews, maintaining strong capitalization and institutional prime-broker relationships.
- Daily mark-to-market reconciliation guarantees that excess margins are monitored and rebalanced.

### 8.3 Algorithmic Margin Calls & Automated Stop-Out
To protect both the client and the clearing bridge from market deficit:
- **Margin Call Level (100%):** When account equity drops to 100% of required margin, the client receives automated platform notifications and email alerts.
- **Stop-Out Level (50%):** When equity drops to 50% of required margin, the execution engine automatically liquidates open positions sequentially, starting with the largest losing position, until account margin requirements are restored.

### 8.4 Negative Balance Protection (NBP)
12 Capital provides retail and professional clients with statutory **Negative Balance Protection**. In black-swan market gaps where liquidity ceases momentarily, client balances are algorithmically prevented from dropping below zero. 12 Capital maintains an internal Risk Reserve Fund to absorb residual anomalies, ensuring the Company never seeks legal recourse against clients for negative balances.
